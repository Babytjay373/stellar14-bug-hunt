# Content Crew

Not coding today? You are the Content Crew. Your job is to make the whole internet feel like it missed something. Everything you make promotes the event and can end up in the official wrap video.

**Hashtag for the day:** `#Stellar14Accra`
**Tag:** @StellarGhana and @BuildOnStellar on X, Stellar Ghana on LinkedIn.

## Roles

Pick one at the briefing. Two people can share a role.

| Role | Your one line brief | Prompts and shot ideas |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hype Reporter | Vlog the day and grab 20 second interviews. | "Why did you come today?" · "What did your team just fix?" · Walk and talk through the room as a round starts |
| Explain It Like I'm Five | Pair with one team and make a 30 second explainer of the bug they fixed, in plain words. | "What was broken, in one sentence?" · "How would you explain it to your mum?" · Before and after shot of the red error turning green |
| First PR Cam | Catch faces the moment a PR gets merged on the projector. | Film the face, then the screen, then the applause · Ask "How does it feel?" right after · Vertical, 10 to 30 seconds |
| Live Tweeter | Post round updates as a thread from one account. | Round starts: which issues unlocked · Round ends: top 3 teams · One quote from the room per round |
| Photographer | Own the photos. | Wide room shot with the projector · Hands on keyboards · Team huddles and high fives · The group photo at the end |
| Meme Lord | Turn moments from the room into code memes. | The borrow checker saying no · "It compiles" faces · Waiting for CI to go green |
| Scoreboard MC Sidekick | Help the host run the scoreboard and hype the teams. | Read the scores between rounds · Interview the leading team · Count down the last 10 seconds out loud |
| Street Interviewer | At the break, take the mic around the room and outside. | "Explain open source in five words" · "Jollof or waakye?" quick fire · "Who is your MVP today?" |
| Reel Editor | Stitch a 45 second recap before the close. | Collect the best clips from the crew by the start of the final round · Hook in the first 2 seconds · End on the merged shout or the scoreboard |

## Your own first PR

You can get a real open source contribution merged today without installing anything. Everything works in the GitHub web editor.

| Issue | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| #17 | Add yourself to the attendees wall: one new file in `attendees/` |
| #18 | Fix one typo in `GLOSSARY.md` |
| #19 | Write a plain English definition for one TODO term in `GLOSSARY.md` |

How: open the file on GitHub, click the pencil icon, make your change, click **Commit changes**, then **Propose changes** and **Create pull request**. GitHub makes the fork and the branch for you. Title your PR `Crew: <what you did>`, for example `Crew: define Fork in the glossary`.

## The Content Crew competition

* **Best piece of content**, judged live at the close. The finalists are shown on the projector and the room votes with applause. The host breaks a tie.
* **Most engagement**: whichever post with `#Stellar14Accra` has the most likes plus reposts by 9pm tonight gets a shoutout from the Stellar Ghana account.
* Prize: to be announced on the day.

## Clip drop plan

1. Join the **Stellar 14 Content Crew** WhatsApp group (the QR is at the check-in table).
2. Send your raw clips and photos there **as documents**, so WhatsApp does not crush the quality. Name them `team-or-role_what-it-is` if you can.
3. Hard deadline for the Reel Editor: the start of the final round.
4. Everything you send may be reused in the official wrap video, with credit.
5. Ask before filming anyone who looks unsure, and skip anyone who says no.
